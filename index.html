The Props

On stilts beside the harbour

Known locally as the Props — the house on stilts. A historic bed and breakfast and restaurant next to Polperro harbour, among the fishermen’s cottages.

Beams and masts from the Maverine, which foundered around 1700, sit in the building, with nautical and smuggling artefacts throughout.
